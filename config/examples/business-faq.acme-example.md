## 4. Scheduling & Appointment Policies

- **Walk-in Policy:** Walk-ins are accepted on a first-come, first-served basis for quick inspections and tire pressure checks, but appointments are strongly encouraged for oil changes, brake work, and scheduled diagnostics.
- **Cancellation & Rescheduling:** We appreciate at least 24 hours advance notice for cancellations. There is no cancellation fee.
- **Late Arrivals:** If arriving more than 15 minutes past your scheduled appointment time, we will do our best to accommodate your vehicle, but service may be rescheduled if the schedule is full.
- **Loaner Vehicles & Shuttle:** We offer complimentary customer shuttle rides within a 10-mile radius for services lasting more than 2 hours. Enterprise rental car discount is available on-site.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q: How long does an oil change usually take?
**A:** Our standard oil change service takes approximately 35 to 45 minutes, including our complimentary 21-point vehicle safety inspection and tire pressure check.

### Q: Do I get a warranty on repairs and parts?
**A:** Yes! All parts and labor are backed by our 24-Month / 24,000-Mile Nationwide Peace of Mind Warranty.

### Q: Can you inspect my check engine light?
**A:** Yes, we provide check engine light code scans. Minor initial code scanning is complimentary. If in-depth electrical diagnostic testing is required, our multi-point diagnostic fee applies.

### Q: Do you service European or hybrid vehicles?
**A:** Yes, our ASE-certified technicians service domestic, Asian, European (BMW, Audi, Mercedes, VW), and hybrid/electric vehicles.

### Q: Where can I wait during my service?
**A:** We provide a comfortable, quiet customer waiting lounge with free high-speed Wi-Fi, premium coffee, cold beverages, and workstations.
